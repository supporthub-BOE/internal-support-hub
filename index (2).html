Product at a glance
Renesia Hair Renewal Support
Pumpkin Seed Oil + Saw Palmetto · Dietary supplement · 60 vegan softgels · 30-day supply
Full guide (PDF)
2,000 mgPumpkin Seed OilCold-pressed. Phytosterols and essential fatty acids.
320 mgSaw PalmettoCO2-extracted, standardized to 85%+ fatty acids.
15 mgLycopeneLyc-O-Mato, antioxidant support.

How to take it

Two softgels, once a day, with the meal that has the most fat (usually lunch or dinner). The actives are fat-soluble, so taking them with just coffee wastes part of the dose. Consistency matters most.

Who it's for

Mostly women in perimenopause and menopause with gradual, hormone-related thinning at the part, temples and crown.

What customers should expect

  1. Days 1–30 · The quiet reset. Probably nothing visible yet. Shedding may continue or briefly tick up — that's the old cycle finishing.
  2. Days 31–60 · Shedding slows. Fewer hairs in the drain and brush, usually around weeks 5–8.
  3. Days 61–90 · First sprouts. Short “baby hairs” at the temples and part line.
  4. Months 4–6. Length and density that other people notice.
  5. Months 6–12. The compounding phase.
Rules for agents
  • Never promise results or give medical advice. Use the guide's wording: judge progress at day 90, with photos rather than feelings.
  • Pregnant or nursing: they should skip it. Taking HRT, hormone-sensitive or blood-thinning medication: they should check with their prescriber first.
  • Recommend a doctor or dermatologist for patchy loss, a red/painful/scaly scalp, sudden massive shedding, or signs of scarring.
  • These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. The product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Customer FAQs
From the 90-Day Hair Renewal Guide
A customer missed a dose. Did they ruin it?

No. Take the next one as scheduled and don't double up. Results come from the month, not the day.

Can they take it with HRT or other medications?

Many women do, but that's a conversation for their prescriber. Ask them to bring the label (pumpkin seed oil, saw palmetto, lycopene) to their next appointment. Skip it if pregnant or nursing, and get the green light first if on hormone-sensitive or blood-thinning medication.

When will they see results?

Shedding usually slows in month two, baby hairs show in month three, and other people notice length and density in months four to six.

Shedding hasn't calmed by week six. Should they stop?

No. They'd be quitting inside the window where change is still arriving. Judge at day 90, with photos. A shed can also echo a stressful event from about three months ago.

Shedding got worse in the first weeks. Is that normal?

It can happen. The hairs falling now were already scheduled to shed months ago — it's the old cycle finishing, not the new one failing.

How should they take it?

Two softgels once a day with their highest-fat meal, at the same time every day.

Do they have to do all the habits in the guide?

No. Softgels, the five-minute scalp massage and the photo tracking are the core. The rest is optional.

What happens after 90 days?

Keep going. The best results in the research sit at month six and beyond.

When should they see a professional?

Patchy loss, an angry or painful scalp, sudden massive shedding, signs of scarring, or symptoms like exhaustion, unexplained weight change or very heavy periods.

Not medical advice. For anything about a customer's health, medications or conditions, refer them to their doctor.
